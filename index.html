A SIMPLE WAY TO MAKE AN IMPACT

How FoodBridge works

Three simple steps turn extra food into an opportunity to help.

🍲
01

Restaurants donate

Food donors list safe surplus food with quantity, collection deadline and pickup details.

🔎
02

NGOs discover

Registered NGOs browse available donations and find food they can collect for their communities.

💚
03

Food reaches people

Donations are collected and distributed, helping reduce waste and support people in need.
